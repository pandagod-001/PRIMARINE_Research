# PRIMARINE Research Prototype — Operational & Research Limitations

**Document Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Authoritative  

---

## 1. Prototype Limitations (What it Does NOT Do)

1. **No Live Data Connections:** Does not stream live market indices, Baltic spot rates, or commodity futures.
2. **No Real-Time Vessel Telemetry:** Does not track ships or stream live AIS satellite data.
3. **No Live Port API Integration:** Does not query port authority berth schedules or bathymetric draft restrictions.
4. **No Autonomous Contract Execution:** Does not execute charter parties, fixing notices, or financial freight agreements.
5. **No Guarantee of Invariant Deployment Coverage:** Conformal prediction guarantees finite-sample marginal coverage under exchangeability; severe macroeconomic covariate shifts degrade empirical bounds.
6. **Not the Complete Enterprise System:** Demonstrates only the validated uncertainty-gated decision mechanism.
